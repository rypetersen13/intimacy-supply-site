What you get

  1. Up to 34% off everythingVIP pricing applies automatically on every order. No codes.
  2. One Member Token every monthEach token covers up to $50 toward an item. Pricier items use more tokens ($60 uses 2, $189 uses 4). Tokens expire 12 months after they are issued.
  3. Skip any monthSkip between the 1st and 5th and you are not charged. Your tokens carry forward.

How billing works

You are charged $39.95 on the 6th of each month for your membership benefits unless you skip. To skip, sign in, open My Account, then Overview, and tap Skip This Month. Your card statement shows Dharma Media & Technology LLC, never Intimacy Supply.

Cancel any time online, or call or text (559) 334-0826. There are no cancellation fees. Unused tokens stay usable for 60 days after you cancel. Full details are in the Terms of Service and the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
