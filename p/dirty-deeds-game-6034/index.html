Sex is Only Dirty When You do it Right! Little Genie Productions brings us the Dirty Deeds Game that shows everyone, even the clean-cut, how to play dirty. How to Play: Separate the cards into four decks (Getting dirty, Even Dirtier, Doing the Deed, and the Items) and place the activity card decks face down. Set the Item cards to face up on the table so that all eight items are in plain view. Decide who will play first. Play begins by drawing a card from the Getting Dirty deck. After an activity is drawn, the drawing player can choose any one of the eight items to incorporate into the performance of the activity. Each activity has an assigned point value. Players can only advance to the next level once they attain 50 points, after which time they may draw from the Even Dirtier card deck. The first player to reach 100 points wins the game and may then draw a card from the Doing the Deed card deck and perform the activity as described. Only one card may be removed per player per turn.
