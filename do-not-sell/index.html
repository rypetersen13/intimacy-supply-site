Do Not Sell My Personal Information

Dharma Media & Technology LLC - Last Updated: May 2026

We do not sell your personal information. Dharma Media & Technology LLC (Intimacy Supply) does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California residents have the right to:

1. Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell.
2. Delete personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions.
3. Opt Out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.
4. Non-Discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights.

How to Submit a Request

To submit a data access, deletion, or opt-out request:

Email: hello@intimacysupply.com (subject: CCPA Request)
Phone/Text: (559) 334-0826
We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days as required by law.

Data We Collect

Name, email, shipping address, date of birth, order history, account preferences, and usage data. We share this only with payment processors, shipping partners, and analytics providers (Google Analytics, Klaviyo) as necessary to operate the business.
